# Attendance Requirement

## General Rule
- Attendance of **70%** is required for theoretical courses and **80%** for applied/lab courses.
- A student who does not meet the attendance requirement cannot sit the final exam and fails the course due to absence (NA).

## Attendance Tracking
- Taking attendance is the instructor's responsibility; the method is announced at the start of the course.
- Students are responsible for tracking their own attendance on SIS.

## Excuses
- Documented excuses (medical report, official duty, etc.) are submitted to the relevant board.
- Acceptance of an excuse does not automatically remove the absence limit; the decision rests with the board.

## FAQ
- A course failed due to absence is retaken the next time it is offered.
- Absence in one course does not affect the status of other courses.

## Reference
Education and Examination Regulation – Article 15
