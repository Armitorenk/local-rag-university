# Graduation Requirements

## General Requirements
- Pass all mandatory and required elective courses in the program.
- Complete the total ECTS credits (usually 240 ECTS for a bachelor's degree).
- Have a Grade Point Average (GPA) of at least **2.00**.

## Mandatory Components
- Any mandatory internship(s) completed and approved.
- In programs with a graduation project/thesis, the project is successfully submitted.

## Graduation Process
1. After the final semester's grades are finalized, graduation status is checked.
2. Library, dormitory, and laboratory clearance is completed.
3. The diploma and diploma supplement are issued.

## Honor and High Honor
- GPA 3.00–3.49: Honor student.
- GPA 3.50 and above: High Honor student.
- Students with a disciplinary penalty cannot be on these lists.

## Reference
Education and Examination Regulation – Article 22
