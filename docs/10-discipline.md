# Student Disciplinary Rules

## Scope
The Higher Education Student Disciplinary Regulation defines the rules students must follow and the corresponding penalties.

## Penalty Types
- Warning
- Reprimand
- Suspension from the institution for one week to one month
- Suspension for one or two semesters
- Expulsion from the higher education institution

## Common Disciplinary Offences
- Cheating or attempting to cheat in an exam.
- Taking an exam in place of someone else, or having someone take it for you.
- Producing forged documents.
- Violence, insult, or damage to property on campus.

## Process
1. The incident is reported to management and an investigation is opened.
2. The student's written defense is requested.
3. The board's decision is served to the student, with the right of appeal stated.

## Important
- Disciplinary penalties are recorded in the student's file and may affect rights such as scholarships and honor lists.
