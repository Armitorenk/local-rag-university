# Internship Procedures

## General
- The internship lasts the number of days required by the program (usually 20–40 working days).
- Internships are usually done in summer, when there are no classes.

## Finding a Placement
1. The student finds the host organization themselves.
2. An internship acceptance letter is obtained from the organization.
3. The internship committee approves the suitability of the placement.

## Application Documents
- Internship application / acceptance form.
- Mandatory internship insurance (SGK) is arranged by the university; the student must submit the form on time.
- Copy of ID and bank IBAN information.

## After the Internship
- The internship report and the organization's evaluation form are submitted on time.
- The internship committee reviews the report and accepts or rejects the internship.
- An accepted internship is recorded as "Passed" on the transcript.
