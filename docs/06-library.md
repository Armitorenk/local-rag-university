# Library Services

## Opening Hours
- The library is open 08:30–22:00 on weekdays and 10:00–18:00 on weekends.
- During final exam periods, 24/7 study halls are opened.

## Borrowing
- Undergraduate students may borrow up to 5 books at a time for 15 days.
- The period can be renewed online twice if there is no other request.
- Reserve and reference materials cannot be taken outside the library.

## Late Fees
- A daily late fee applies for each book returned after the due date.
- A student with overdue items cannot borrow new books or complete clearance.

## Digital Resources
- Databases and e-journals can be accessed from the campus network or via VPN.
- Group study rooms are used by online reservation.
