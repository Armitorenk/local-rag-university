# Scholarships and Financial Aid

## Types
- Merit scholarship: based on GPA and ranking.
- Need-based scholarship: based on income status.
- Meal scholarship: provides daily meal support.
- Part-time work: paid on-campus work opportunity for students.

## Application
1. Scholarship applications open on announced dates at the start of the fall semester.
2. Applications are made online through the Department of Health, Culture and Sports (SKS).
3. Required documents: income statement, transcript, residence certificate, and application form.

## Merit Scholarship Conditions
- GPA must be at least 3.00.
- No disciplinary penalty.
- Must be within the normal study period.

## Cancellation
- A scholarship may be cancelled if the GPA falls below the threshold, a disciplinary penalty is received, or enrollment is frozen.
