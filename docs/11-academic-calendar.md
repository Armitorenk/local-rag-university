# Academic Calendar and Leave of Absence

## Academic Calendar
- The academic year has two semesters: fall and spring.
- Each semester covers about 14 weeks of classes.
- Course registration, add-drop, midterms, finals, resits, and official holidays are announced in the calendar.

## Leave of Absence (Freezing Enrollment)
- Enrollment can be frozen for justified reasons such as health, financial, family, or military service.
- The application is made to the relevant dean's office/directorate with a supporting document.
- The frozen period does not count toward the maximum study period.

## Durations
- Enrollment can usually be frozen for at most two semesters at a time.
- The total freeze duration over the whole study is limited by the regulation.

## Returning
1. When the leave ends, the student registers for courses following the normal calendar.
2. Courses missed during the freeze are taken when they are next offered.

## Reference
Education and Examination Regulation – Article 18
