# Course Registration

## Purpose
Each semester, students must register for the courses they will take.

## Registration Period
- Registration is done online through the Student Information System (SIS) on the dates in the academic calendar.
- Registration renewal opens about one week before the semester starts.
- The add-drop week is during the first week of classes.

## Steps
1. Log in to SIS with your student ID and password.
2. Pay any tuition or contribution fee; registration will not open until payment is made.
3. Add your required (mandatory) and elective courses to the cart.
4. Make sure the total credits do not exceed the semester limit (usually 30 ECTS).
5. Submit for advisor approval.

## Advisor Approval
- Registration is not complete until your academic advisor approves the selected courses.
- For unapproved registrations, you must contact your advisor.

## Notes
- Failed courses (FF/FD) from previous semesters must be taken first.
- Time-conflicting courses are blocked by the system.

## Reference
Undergraduate Education and Examination Regulation – Article 8
