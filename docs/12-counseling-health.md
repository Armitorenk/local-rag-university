# Counseling and Health Services

## Psychological Counseling Center (PDR)
- Provides free and confidential individual counseling to students.
- Supports topics such as exam anxiety, adaptation, motivation, relationships, and stress.
- Appointments are made through the online system or by applying to the center.

## Health Service (Mediko)
- The campus health unit provides first aid and basic examination.
- In emergencies, referral to the nearest hospital is arranged.

## Confidentiality
- Session contents are confidential and not shared without the student's consent.
- Exceptional cases such as risk of harm to self or others are subject to the regulation.

## Disabled Student Unit
- Handles requests for accessibility, extra exam time, and suitable materials.
