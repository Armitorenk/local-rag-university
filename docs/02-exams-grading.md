# Exams and Grading System

## Exam Types
- Midterm: held in the middle of the semester.
- Final: held at the end of the semester.
- Make-up (resit) exam: for students who failed or could not sit the final.
- Excuse exam: for students with a valid, documented excuse.

## How the Grade Is Calculated
- The course grade is usually 40% midterm + 60% final.
- To pass a course you need at least a **CC** letter grade or a final score of at least 60.
- The Grade Point Average (GPA) is calculated out of 4.00.

## Letter Grades
- AA = 4.0, BA = 3.5, BB = 3.0, CB = 2.5, CC = 2.0
- DC = 1.5, DD = 1.0 (conditional pass), FD = 0.5, FF = 0.0 (fail)

## Grade Appeal
1. Within 5 working days after grades are announced, you may appeal a clerical error.
2. The appeal is submitted to the relevant unit by petition.
3. Only calculation/clerical errors are reviewed.
