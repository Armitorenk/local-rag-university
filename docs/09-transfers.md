# Horizontal and Vertical Transfers

## Horizontal Transfer
- A transfer from another university or another program within the same university.
- Possible by GPA or by central placement score (Additional Article 1).
- Applications are taken within the announced quota and dates.

## Horizontal Transfer Conditions
- A minimum GPA between 2.50 and 3.00 is usually required.
- There must be no disciplinary penalty.
- Transfer is usually not allowed for the first semester of the first year or for the final year.

## Vertical Transfer (DGS)
- Transfer of associate-degree (2-year) graduates to related bachelor's programs.
- Placement is made via the DGS exam held by the central body (ÖSYM).
- An adjustment (intibak) program may be applied.

## Course Adjustment / Exemption
1. Exemption requests for courses taken in the previous program are made by petition.
2. The adjustment committee determines equivalent courses.
3. Approved courses do not need to be retaken.
