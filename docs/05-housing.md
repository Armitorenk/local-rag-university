# Housing and Dormitories

## Application
- Dormitory applications are taken online at the start of the academic year.
- Placement considers distance, income status, and academic score.
- Newly enrolled first-year students are usually given priority.

## Fees and Deposit
- The dormitory fee can be paid monthly or per semester in advance.
- A refundable deposit is collected at entry and returned at checkout if there is no damage.

## Rules
1. Entries and exits are logged by card system.
2. Late-night entries must follow the designated hours.
3. High-power devices such as heaters and stoves are forbidden in rooms.
4. Hosting guests is subject to management approval.

## Leaving the Dormitory
- To leave, you must submit a petition at least 15 days in advance.
- Refund of the remaining fee is made according to the directive.
